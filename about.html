THE CHALLENGE

The Problem Statement

Traditional art commerce often restricts emerging independent creators due to prohibitive intermediary gallery commissions, lack of transparent provenance, and opaque auction mechanisms. Collectors conversely struggle to discover genuine artworks across varied disciplines with verifiable pricing and trustworthy acquisition channels.

✨
THE INNOVATION

The ArtLoom Solution

ArtLoom provides a dignified, curated online atelier where artists manage their portfolios directly, collectors acquire pieces seamlessly or participate in competitive auctions with instant notifications, and administrators maintain catalog authenticity. Built with robust vanilla architecture for frictionless clientside experience.
