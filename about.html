Academic Project Overview

About ArtLoom Online Art Gallery

ArtLoom is a comprehensive web-based platform engineered specifically for the SDC Project Review-1. It enables art collectors, independent artists, and administrators to interact within a complete digital art business ecosystem.

🎯

The Problem Statement

Traditional art commerce often restricts emerging independent creators due to high intermediary gallery fees, lack of digital exhibition avenues, and opaque auction mechanisms. Collectors conversely struggle to discover genuine artworks across varied categories with transparent pricing and live bidding.

💡

The ArtLoom Solution

ArtLoom provides a decentralized-style online gallery where artists manage their portfolios, collectors discover and acquire works or participate in live auctions, and administrators maintain catalog integrity. All operations are simulated seamlessly clientside.
